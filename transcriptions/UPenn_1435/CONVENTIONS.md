## The manuscript
- University of Pennsylvania, Ms. Codex 1435: Florentine household/building accounts in mercantesca, 1460s (e.g. ".1466.").
- Images: images/UPenn_1435/ms1435_imgNNN.jpg; images/UPenn_1435/index.csv gives folio per image (folio numbers read from the
  pages; they have jumps). Element 2 = f. 3r.

## What transfers from MPAP 4 / MS 493 — and what does not
- TRANSFERS: Florentine account vocabulary and usage (a di …, ꝓ, ī tucto, quaderno … a c., lire … soldi … denari).
  reference/MPAP4_vocabulary.md and Edler's word lists help recognise words; they are not evidence of this scribe's spelling.
- DOES NOT TRANSFER: the other hands, and their money glyphs.

## Observed in the ground truth (to be confirmed by Laura)
- Lira = 𝓎 (e.g. "___𝓎 12 β 3 𝒹"). Not ⫳, not ℔ (℔ occurs twice: check what it means here).
- Soldi: mostly β in the sums column ("𝓎 33 β ii 𝒹"), but ß also occurs ("lire octo ß 10"). OPEN QUESTION: one sign or two?
- Sums are written out in words and repeated in figures: "lire trente tre β xi ___ 𝓎 33 β ii 𝒹".
- Ruled dashes before the sum: ___ (as elsewhere).
- Struck-through text is encoded with combining strike-through characters (1̶4̶6̶0̶). OPEN QUESTION: convert to [del: 1460]
  (the project convention), or keep?
- Roman numerals in the GT end in i, not j (8 cases seen); arabic figures are frequent in dates and sums.

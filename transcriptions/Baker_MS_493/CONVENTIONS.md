## The manuscript
- Baker Library (Harvard Business School), Medici MS 493: a Florentine account book, 1419–1421 and after.
- Images: images/Baker_MS_493/ms493_imgNNN.jpg. Folio n recto = img 2n−4 (7r = img010, 14r = img024, 21r = img038, 21v = img039).
- Edler (de Roover), Glossary of Mediaeval Terms of Business (1934) quotes this manuscript: reference/glossary/edler_ms493_quotes.json
  (folio → image). Edler's text is normalised (word division, some expansions): use it to identify words, then render them
  diplomatically, as written here.

## What transfers from MPAP 4 — and what does not
- TRANSFERS: vocabulary and usage of Florentine account books (de avere / deono avere adi …, a uscita, a entrata, libro …
  a carta, posto a …, the money of account ƒ · lira · ß · 𝒹, rates "a ß 80 ꝓ ƒ"). reference/MPAP4_vocabulary.md is a word list
  from MPAP 4, useful for recognising words: it is NOT evidence of how this scribe writes them.
- DOES NOT TRANSFER: the MPAP 4 hand. Its palaeographical notes (ʒ-shaped figure = 3, capital R looking like B, the
  look of its ℔, Y-shaped final = ii) and its hand-specific rules do not apply here unless confirmed on MS 493.
- The shared rule sets adopted for this manuscript start as "check only" (⚠ to look at, no automatic fixes).

## Observed in Laura's corrections so far
- Lira (money) = ⫳, e.g. "ƒ uno ⫳ due", "ƒ 1⫳ ii ß". Never convert ⫳ to ℔.
- ℔ = the WEIGHT pound (libbra) in this manuscript (Laura, 18 Oct): "ꝓ ℔ una e mezo di polvere", "℔ quindici di pinochiato".
  The lira is always ⫳. Never convert one to the other; a ℔ inside a money sum (ƒ … ℔ … ß) is flagged for checking (rule lira-is-not-libbra).
- Roman numerals end in j where the scribe writes j: xij, xxvij, xxj (unlike MPAP 4, where final i is the rule). Keep as written.
  Year headings: + Mccccº xviiiiº, Mccccº xxj. Years in dates are usually arabic: 1419, 1420, 1421.
- Florin: ƒ alone (the stroke after it is part of the symbol), as for MPAP 4 (Laura, 18 Oct) — to be confirmed for this hand.
- Abbreviations seen: ꝓ, ꝑ, ꝙ (quale: "il ꝙaule"), ī, on̄, Mō (monna), g° (grossi), novemƀe, liƀo, ⁊, º.
- Exchange formula: "ī quatrini a ß 80 ꝓ ƒ" (in quattrini at 80 soldi per florin).
